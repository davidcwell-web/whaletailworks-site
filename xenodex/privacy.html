XENODEX - Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026 — Applies to the XENODEX game app (all platforms)

The short version

XENODEX collects no personal data. None. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours that the game talks to.

Data the game stores

Your game progress (score, level, coins, squad, bases, xenodex intel) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or choosing NEW OPERATION deletes it.

Network access

The game is fully playable offline. If a connection is available, the game may download static, cosmetic texture images (for example, photographic planet surface maps) from public content delivery networks. These requests contain no personal information, no identifiers, and no game data - they are ordinary image downloads, identical for every player. Without a connection the game substitutes built-in graphics automatically.

Data we collect, share, or sell

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any data. There is no third-party SDK, no advertising network, and no analytics library in the app.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. The game contains mild science-fiction fantasy violence and is a work of fiction.

Permissions

The app requests no special device permissions - no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage beyond its own app data.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of the above (for example, adding online leaderboards), this policy will be updated first and the store listing's Data Safety section will be revised to match.

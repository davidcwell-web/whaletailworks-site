Delete your data — Wickhold

Whale Tail Works LLC · Effective 2026-11-01

Wickhold is made by Whale Tail Works LLC. This page explains how to delete the data the game sends to our servers, what is deleted, and what is kept. Full details are in the Wickhold privacy policy.

Step 1: Remove your published scores (in the app)

  1. Open Wickhold on the same device you used to publish your scores.
  2. Go to the Leaderboard and choose the option to remove your published scores.

This deletes the leaderboard entries linked to that device's ownership token. It works only while that token is still on the device. If you cleared the app's data or uninstalled the app, use Step 2.

Step 2: Ask us to delete the rest

Email support@whaletailworks.net with the subject line "Wickhold data deletion". Include:

  • the callsign you used (for example, the name on your leaderboard entry or invitation)
  • the rough date you played or published a score
  • the name of the device you used, if you remember it

We will remove any matching records we can find and reply to confirm. Do not send your device token or any invitation code you do not want shared. We cannot always match a record to you, because we store only a hashed form of the token.

What is deleted

  • Your published leaderboard entries and their statistics.
  • Callsigns and results stored with your match records, when we can match them to you.

What is kept, and for how long

  • On your device: saves, settings, your local records and the ownership token. Uninstalling the app removes them. Clearing the app's data in your phone's Settings also removes them.
  • Match rooms and invitations: they stop working seven days after they are created. They are removed only when a cleanup runs during later service requests, so a closed room can remain in the database for longer than seven days. We do not promise a fixed date.
  • Server connection logs: our hosting provider (Supabase) keeps request logs, which can include IP address and approximate city and country. These are kept under the provider's own retention settings.

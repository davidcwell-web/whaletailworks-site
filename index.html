Small Studio. Big Worlds.

Whale Tail Works LLC is a one-developer independent studio publishing original games and running a curated online storefront — every game designed, coded, and shipped with obsessive care.

Original Games

From alien-hunting strategy to cozy pixel tactics — premium games with no ads, no gacha, no nonsense. Pay once, play forever.

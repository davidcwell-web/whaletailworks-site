Reef Currents

Privacy Policy · Whale Tail Works LLC · Applies from the first public release of Reef Currents on Google Play

The short version

Reef Currents is free to play and shows an occasional advertisement between puzzles. Ads are non-personalised and treated as directed to children. One optional purchase removes ads permanently. The game has no accounts, no analytics of our own, no chat, no links out to the web, and your progress never leaves your device.

What the game stores on your device

Your puzzle progress, reef journal, settings (sound, music, motion, low-power) and simple local play statistics such as how many turns a board took are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free version shows interstitial advertisements between puzzles through Google AdMob. We configure AdMob to treat every player as a child for advertising purposes and to request non-personalised ads only, with content rated for general audiences. Because every player is treated as a child, the app does not request Android's advertising-identifier permission and no advertising identifier is provided to Google. AdMob may still process the technical information Google describes for non-personalised advertising, such as your IP address, coarse location derived from it, and ad-interaction events, under Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive that information. Under the child-directed configuration no personalised-advertising consent is ever requested; where a regional notice is still required, it is shown before any ad.

Ads never appear during a puzzle, at launch, or after you use undo or a hint. Hints and undo are free and unlimited.

Purchases

A single optional purchase, "Remove ads", stops all advertising and stops the game loading the advertising software at all. Google Play handles the payment under its own privacy policy; the game only receives confirmation that the purchase exists and stores that fact locally so it works offline. There are no other purchases, no currencies and no subscriptions.

Children

Reef Currents is suitable for all ages and is offered in Google Play's Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Advertising is non-personalised for every player. There are no social features and nothing in the game can open a browser or contact another person.

Network access

The free version requests internet access solely to load advertisements and to verify the remove-ads purchase. The game works fully offline; without a connection it simply shows no ads.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, this policy and the Play Store data-safety section will be updated before that version is released.

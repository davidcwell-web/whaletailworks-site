Reef Currents

Privacy Policy · Whale Tail Works LLC · iOS version · Applies from the first release on the App Store

The short version

Reef Currents is a paid puzzle game with no advertising, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics. It does not connect to the internet, so it works fully offline. Your progress stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

What the game stores on your device

Your puzzle progress, reef journal, settings (sound, music, motion, low-power) and simple local play statistics, such as how many turns a board took, are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The iOS version contains no advertising software and shows no advertisements. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track you.

Purchases

The iOS version is a single paid download through the App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. The game has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or currencies, and it does not receive or store any payment information.

Network access

The iOS version makes no network requests. Everything it needs, including fonts, artwork and sound, is inside the app. Your device's connection is never used by the game.

Children

Reef Currents has no accounts, no chat, no social features and no links out to the web. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and it has no advertising, so no child is shown ads or tracked.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app collects or shares, this policy will be updated before that version is released. A separate policy covers the Android version of Reef Currents.

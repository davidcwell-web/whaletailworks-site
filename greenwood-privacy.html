Guardians of the Greenwood Way

Privacy Policy · Whale Tail Works LLC · Effective 6 September 2026

The short version

The game collects no personal data. It does not use the internet. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party services.

What the game stores

Your progress — battles, campaign position, unlocked guardians, gear, medals and settings such as sound levels, language and whether you have seen the tutorial — is saved on your device only, in the app's local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Network access

The app does not request the Android internet permission and makes no network connections. Fonts, artwork and sound are bundled inside the app.

Children

The game is rated for ages 10 and up. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Purchases

The game is sold once, up front, through Google Play. Google handles that transaction under its own privacy policy; the game itself never sees payment details. There are no in-app purchases.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that collects data, this policy and the Play Store data-safety section will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Whale Tail Works LLC — contact through the developer page on Google Play, or via whaletailworks.net.

This policy covers the Android application Guardians of the Greenwood Way. The company-wide policy is at whaletailworks.net/privacy.html.

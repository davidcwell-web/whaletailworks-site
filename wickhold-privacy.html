Privacy Policy — Wickhold

Whale Tail Works LLC · Effective 2026-11-01

The short version

Wickhold is a survival game with online multiplayer (Co-op, Versus and Ghost Race for 2–4 players) and an optional public leaderboard. Solo play and your saves stay on your device. Online modes send your callsign and match data to a server. We don't run accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't sell data.

What stays on your device

Solo saves, settings, workshop progress, your local records history and a random capability token are stored in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What is sent to our server

  • Callsign. The name you type. Other players in your match see it. It appears on the public leaderboard only if you publish a solo run.
  • Match data. Invitation codes, the match state needed to play (positions and actions during a live match are relayed in real time), and results when a match ends.
  • Published solo runs. Only when you press Publish: your callsign and run statistics (destinations, takedowns, survival time, difficulty and similar counters). Nothing is published automatically.
  • Your ownership token. A random 256-bit value created on your device. The server stores only a SHA-256 hash of it, which lets you withdraw your own published entries. It is not linked to your name or to any account.
  • Connection data. Our hosting provider (Supabase) keeps request logs for the service. These can include your IP address, device user-agent, approximate city and country inferred from the IP address, and request timing. The game also uses an IP-derived value to rate-limit requests, held under a hashed key. We do not use GPS, and the game does not ask for location permission. Connection data is not used to identify you.

Who processes it

We use Supabase to host the database and the real-time relay. Supabase acts as our service provider. The same Supabase project also hosts another Whale Tail Works game; each game's data is kept in its own private database schema.

Retention

  • Match rooms and invitations stop working seven days after they are created. They are deleted only when a cleanup runs, which happens during later service requests, so an expired room may remain in the database for longer. We do not promise a fixed deletion date.
  • Published solo runs stay on the leaderboard until you withdraw them, or until we remove them under this policy.

Your choices and deletion

  • Do not press Publish if you don't want a run shown publicly.
  • To withdraw your published runs, use the in-game leaderboard option on the same device. This removes only the leaderboard entries linked to that device's token. It does not delete match history, server logs or local saves on your device.
  • If you have cleared the app's data or lost the device, we cannot match your published runs to you, because we hold only a hash of the token. You can ask us to remove entries if you can identify them by callsign and approximate date: contact support@whaletailworks.net.

Children

Wickhold is a zombie survival game and is intended for teens and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game has no text or voice chat. Callsigns are the only text other players see, and we ask players not to use their real name.

Network access

Solo play works offline. Online multiplayer and the leaderboard require an internet connection.

Purchases

Wickhold is a one-time paid app, sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under its own privacy policy. The game itself never sees payment details.

Changes

If a future version collects more data, this policy and the Play Store data-safety section will be updated before that version is published.

Privacy Policy — The Last Residents

Whale Tail Works LLC · Draft for a game in development · 2026-09-20

The short version

The game collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party services. This policy describes the planned release; it will be confirmed before the game is published.

What the game stores

Your progress through the house and your settings (sound, motion, display) are saved on your device only, in the app's private storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Network access

The game is designed to play fully offline. It makes no network connections of its own; every picture, sound and line of text ships inside the app.

Children

The game is a mystery with a spooky atmosphere, intended for teens and up. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Purchases

The game will be sold once, up front, through Google Play. Google handles that transaction under its own privacy policy; the game itself never sees payment details.

Changes

If the released version adds any feature that collects data, this policy and the Play Store data-safety section will be updated before that version is published.

Contact

Whale Tail Works LLC — support@whaletailworks.net, or through the developer page on Google Play. See also the studio privacy policy.

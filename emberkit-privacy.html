Privacy Policy — EMBERKIT

Whale Tail Works LLC · Effective 2026-09-18

The short version

The game collects no personal data. It does not use the internet. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party services.

What the game stores

Your progress (lanterns relit, embers and seeds collected, best times, unlocked moves) and your settings (volume, motion, touch layout) are saved on your device only, in the app's local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, and the Settings screen has an "Erase all progress" button.

Network access

The app does not request the internet permission and makes no network connections. All art, music and sound are generated by the game's own code on your device; there are no downloads.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Purchases

The game is sold once, up front, through Google Play. Google handles that transaction under its own privacy policy; the game itself never sees payment details.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that collects data, this policy and the Play Store data-safety section will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Whale Tail Works LLC — support@whaletailworks.net, or through the developer page on Google Play. See also the studio privacy policy.
